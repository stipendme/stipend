# Stipend brand brief

Name: Stipend. Domain: stipend.my (registered 2026-09-17; stipend.my and stipend.mynance were the earlier candidates).
Tagline: "Stake SOL. Get paid in stocks."
Tokens are named after the asset: nvdaSOL, aaplSOL, goldSOL, spySOL.

Voice: a payslip, not a casino. Calm, exact, slightly dry. Numbers are shown as what you actually receive ("0.41 NVDA per 100 SOL per month"), never as APY-only hype.
Audience: SOL holders who already stake and would rather accumulate a stock or metal position than more SOL. They know what an LST is.
Primary job of the site: pick an asset, mint its LST, come back to claim.
